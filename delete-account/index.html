Delete your PhotoVibe account

Last updated: 2026-10-28
App: PhotoVibe (ai.photovibe.app)
Contact: support@photovibe.ai

You can delete your PhotoVibe account and the data linked to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — From inside the app (fastest)

  • Open PhotoVibe and sign in.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is removed immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Without the app

If you have already uninstalled the app, send an email to support@photovibe.ai from the email address linked to your account, or include the phone number you signed up with. Use the subject line Delete my account. We will confirm your identity by replying to that address or sending a one-time code to that number, then delete the account within 7 days of verification.

What is deleted

  • Your login (phone number or email, and password hash).
  • Your profile, plan, and image allowance counters.
  • Generation audit metadata (image hashes, timestamps, status).
  • Any content reports you submitted.
  • Your subscription link on our side. An active Google Play subscription must be cancelled separately in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions) or it will keep renewing.

Generated images are stored only on your own device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Operational server logs that may reference your user id: up to 30 days, then purged automatically.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Encrypted backups may hold residual references for up to 90 days before they are fully rotated out.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as tax invoices for paid subscriptions issued through Google Play, are retained by Google under its own policy.

For full details see the Privacy Policy.
